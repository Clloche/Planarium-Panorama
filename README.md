# Planarium Panorama

*An addon for [Ars Nouveau](https://github.com/baileyholl/Ars-Nouveau) (NeoForge 1.21.1).*

> [!NOTE]
> **Made with the help AI.** The code was written with the help of **Claude** (Anthropic) and **Codex** (OpenAI).

Ars Nouveau's Planarium lets you shrink yourself into a pocket dimension inside a jar. Normally, the world around
that jar is a black void. Planarium Panorama turns the jar into a window: from inside, you see the area around the
Planarium as a giant world, 32 times bigger, under the real sky, with real lighting!

## Features

Includes : 
- **Giant view:** the blocks around the Planarium, drawn 32 times bigger around the jar.
- **Synced sky and weather:** the outside time, sun, moon, rain, snow, clouds and lightning.
- **Real lighting:** the giant view, the miniature and the jar interior are lit like the Planarium's real spot.
- **Live scenery:** block changes outside (redstone, pistons, doors...) show up in the giant view within seconds.
- **Outside sounds:** explosions, blocks, mobs and jukebox music from around the Planarium, heard from their direction.
- **Sable sub-levels (experimental):** moving structures in the giant scenery and the miniature, including Planariums placed on a moving structure.

Everything can be changed in game: **Mods** > **Planarium Panorama** > **Config** or in the config files.

## Optional integrations

The mods are detected automatically and their integration can be turned on or off in the config : 
- [AmbientSounds](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ambientsounds)
- [Contraption Lights](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/contraption-lights) (Colored light)
- [Dynamic Surroundings](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dynamic-surroundings)
- [Enhanced Celestials 2](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/enhanced-celestials-2-default-lunar-events)

[Puddles & Floods](https://modrinth.com/mod/puddles-floods) is also detected automatically. Its rain puddles and
visual floods are recreated in the giant scenery using the outside weather, terrain, biomes and sea level.
This follows the **Weather** option and Puddles & Floods' own settings. Client-created puddles develop locally;
they do not reproduce the exact age of puddles left outside before entering the jar. Puddles created by the server
are included in the normal scenery snapshots.

Sable support also covers Create Aeronautics structures. Occupied sections are captured separately and their positions
and rotations are synchronized ten times per second. Block changes follow the normal scenery update settings.
With **Outside world sounds** enabled, visible sub-levels are kept loaded for the visitor; the tickets are released
when the visitor leaves, disconnects, disables the feature, or the structure leaves the captured area.
Miniatures also keep the pocket's collision area active while an enabled viewer is within 64 blocks of a loaded jar,
so saved Sable structures can load and remain visible after leaving the pocket. Those tickets expire after three
seconds without a nearby viewer.
Planarium dimension changes also clear the former Sable viewer before its next tracking tick, avoiding stale removal
packets that can hide a structure in the destination dimension with Sable 2.0.4.
The current limit is 256 visible sub-level sections per scene. A section is 16 × 16 × 16 blocks.
This integration is under validation with Sable 2.0.4 and Create Aeronautics 1.3.2.
It currently captures blocks and their block-entity renderers. Particles generated by sub-level blocks are not yet
recreated in the panorama or miniature; visual testing with moving structures is still required.

Run `./gradlew checkSubLevels` (or `gradlew.bat checkSubLevels` on Windows) for the coordinate and network regression
checks. They also run as part of `build` and verify that Sable remains optional.

## Known issues

- [Modonomicon](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/modonomicon) (singleplayer): very rarely, entering a jar fails halfway. Loading takes a long time, then you float in the void without the jar. This comes from a timing bug with Modonomicon. To fix it, just Save and Quit then rejoin the world.
- Mobs: mobs and players around the Planarium are deliberately not shown in the giant scenery or the miniature (paintings, item frames and armor stands are).
- [Shaders (Iris)](https://modrinth.com/mod/iris): The shaders inside the Planarium will not follow the shaders of the outside.
